99/06 Fly fishing for Kahawai with Steeve

Oh! There was a fisherman.

When I was walking down the aisle, a fisherman was on the shore. Surprisingly, he was a fly fisherman!

Will be any luck with his fly rod?

Steeve was retrieving his white streamer with a floating line, because he had lost his sinking line by a trouble.

A Kahawai attacked on his fly!

A nice size kahawai attacked his streamer. It bent Steeve's fly rod so tightly. Fly fishing for kahawai is a great fun!

His smile.

The kahawai was his first catch in New Zealand, and was also his first catch of saltwater fly fishing. He held his catch with his face beaming with smiles.

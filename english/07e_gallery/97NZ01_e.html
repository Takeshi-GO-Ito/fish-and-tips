97/01/12 The first day at the "Mountain River"

Making a plan of our fishing trip.

After 5000 miles of flight, we had finally arrived at Mr. Allison's lodge. We made our fishing plan, then selected some possible flies.

David and a Timber Track

David and I were awaiting a helicopter. Then a timber track came toward us. David talked to the Driver for a while.

The Helicopter has come!

We had a peaceful time at the farm. The helicopter had come. It picked us up, and took us to the "Mountain River" after fifteen minutes of flight.

A big brown trout stayed on the bank of the river.

David and I walked on the bank of the river. The river had risen by the rain fell few days ago.

They walked across the river with shoulder to shoulder.

Mr. and Mrs. Matsunobu and I could not get a fish today. They were crossing the river with hand in hand, shoulder to shoulder. It was the Kiwi style.

06/99 Kahawai fishing

The Rangitoto island in the morning mist.

Last night I searched out a likely good fishing spot on a map of Auckland city. When I arrived at the beach, the Rangitoto Island was floating on the dense mist quietly.

Everybody was enjoying fishing in their own style.

It was June, but the sunny beach was so warm and comfortable.

Can I catch some fish?

Until I got the first catch, I was wondering that I could not catch anything. But before high tide, few kahawai took my metal lure. They were real fighter.

It would be our delicious dinner.

I kept two pan-size kahawai, and cook them. Their powerful fight had turned my heart into a regular kahawai fishing addict.

98/11/28 With endless, but unsure hope.

With endless, unsure hope.

The Boeing 777 started its engines, and moved gently to the runway of the Nagoya international airport. My fishing friend "Nori" and I had already put our backpacks, rods, reels and endless but unsure hope into the compartment.

It was a gentle take off.

We looked down our city. A long fishing trip has started.

At Changi international airport.

Last fishing trip in 1997 was solo, but this time I travelled with Nori. He took many photographs for me.

How do you kill the long waiting time?

How do you kill the long waiting time ? Do you have any good idea? We had to wait for five hours to transfer. So I made some knotted leaders in the lobby of the airport.
